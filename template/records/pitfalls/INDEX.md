# Project pitfalls

Each pitfall is a file, dated in its name: `YYYY-MM-DD-short-subject.md`.
Entries **are not rewritten** — they capture what was true on the day they were
found. Only the table below is rewritten.

What belongs here: anything that cost time and whose **method of discovery applies
again**. The outcome belongs to the project; the method does not, and the method is
what gets written down.

The entry format is described in the `documentation` skill, under
`references/details.md`.
