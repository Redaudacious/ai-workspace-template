# The project's decisions

A decision is a rule or a choice, dated, with its reason and history. The rule itself
sits in `AGENTS.md` or in the code it concerns; here sits why it is so.

Each decision is a file, dated in its name: `YYYY-MM-DD-short-subject.md`. Entries are
not rewritten. A changed decision gets a new entry, and the row of the old one gains
"replaced by". Only the table below is rewritten.

The form of an entry is in the `documentation` skill, at `references/details.md`.

| Date | The decision | Where it applies | Replaced by |
