# The project's work items

A work item is a piece of work with a written spec and plan. It sits in a folder, in
`open/` while open and in `closed/` afterward: `README.md` for humans, the spec, the
plans, and at closing, `closing.md`.

The index is the only part of the record that gets rewritten. Checkboxes in the plans
stay unchecked: the state lives here.

A work item is closed only when every leftover has a destination: done, abandoned with
the reason recorded, or queued in the backlog. The forms are in the `documentation`
skill, in its long reference.
